For targets patches (wizard step 3, Learning Targets & Assessment): learningGoals, successCriteria, formativeAssessment, summativeAssessment, and optionally transferableSkills.
Write goals and criteria against the selectedCurriculumPieces: each goal should be traceable to at least one provided piece, without quoting or inventing official wording or codes.
Goals start with "Students will"; success criteria are student-facing "I can" statements. Use one line per goal or criterion.
Formative assessment describes observations, conversations, or products used during learning; summative assessment describes the evidence of learning at the end.
For transferableSkills, choose only from allowedTargetOptions.transferableSkills, and only the skills the lesson genuinely develops.
